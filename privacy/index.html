Privacy, in plain words

What I collect

  • What you type in the form: your name, email, and your 7 answers.
  • The report files you choose to send me.
  • Emails you send me.

What I never collect

  • Your logins or passwords. I never ask for them.
  • Card numbers on this site.

How I use it

  • To build your Seller Money Map and walk you through it.
  • To email you about your Money Map and our call.
  • Numbers from your store stay yours. I never post them or share them without your written OK.

Who else sees it

  • Nobody buys or rents your data. I do not sell it.
  • The form answers are stored in a private spreadsheet. Files sit in a private folder. Only I can open them.

Your choices

  • Ask me to delete your files and answers. I will, and I will tell you when it is done.
  • Ask to stop emails at any time. Reply "stop" and you are off.
